# Facilitator guide: AI Term Dash

## Purpose

Use this as a quick, low-stakes check of AI vocabulary for AIPP learners around 10 months into their Multiverse course. The questions move from basic terminology to applied and responsible AI concepts.

## Five-minute run sheet

- **0:00–0:30** – Share the GitHub Pages URL and ask everyone to open it.
- **0:30–4:00** – Learners complete the 10 questions individually.
- **4:00–5:00** – Ask learners to share one term they could now explain more clearly.

## Debrief prompts

Choose one if time is tight:

1. Which two terms are most often confused at work, and how would you distinguish them?
2. Where could RAG make an AI response more useful or trustworthy in your organisation?
3. What is the practical difference between an LLM, a chatbot and an AI agent?
4. What check would you use before relying on an AI-generated answer?

## Answer key

1. Artificial intelligence
2. The instruction or input given to the AI
3. Learn patterns from data
4. A chunk of text processed by the model
5. A confident-sounding but false or unsupported output
6. A model trained to predict and generate language
7. Grounding a model with retrieved information before it responds
8. Randomness and variation in output
9. A system that can plan and take actions using tools toward a goal
10. Algorithmic bias

## Facilitation notes

- Keep it low stakes. Scores are a conversation starter, not an assessment.
- Ask for plain-English explanations and workplace examples rather than memorised definitions.
- If learners finish early, pair them and ask each person to explain one advanced term without using jargon.
- Avoid collecting names or screenshots of individual scores unless learners volunteer them.
